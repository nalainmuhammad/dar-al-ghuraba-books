Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our books, ordering, and shipping

📦 Ordering

Ordering is simple! Browse our catalog, find the book you'd like, and click the "Order via WhatsApp" button. This will open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message containing the book title. Just send the message, and our team will confirm your order, provide payment details, and arrange shipping. It's a personal, hassle-free experience!
Absolutely! You can either click the WhatsApp button for each book individually, or simply message us on WhatsApp with a list of all the books you'd like to order. We'll bundle them together and may even offer a discount on combined shipping for multiple items.
We accept multiple payment methods for your convenience, including bank transfer, mobile payments, and cash on delivery (where available). Our team will share the specific payment details with you via WhatsApp after you place your order.
No, there is no minimum order value. You're welcome to order even a single book! However, ordering multiple books at once can help save on shipping costs, and we may offer bundle discounts on larger orders.

🚚 Shipping & Delivery

Shipping times vary depending on your location:

Domestic: 2–5 business days
International: 7–21 business days

We'll provide you with a tracking number once your order is dispatched so you can follow its journey.
Yes! We ship worldwide. International shipping costs vary based on the destination country and the weight of the package. Contact us via WhatsApp with your location, and we'll provide an accurate shipping quote before you commit to the order.
Shipping costs depend on the weight of your order and the delivery destination. We always aim to offer the most competitive rates possible. For exact costs, just reach out to us on WhatsApp with your order details and delivery address, and we'll calculate the shipping for you.

✅ Book Authenticity

Authenticity is at the core of everything we do. Every book in our collection is sourced directly from reputable publishers and verified for its adherence to the Quran and authenticated Sunnah. We only carry titles that are recommended or authored by recognized Islamic scholars. Our name, "Dar Al Ghuraba" (Home of the Strangers), is a promise of quality and trust.
All our books are 100% original, sourced directly from authorized publishers. We do not sell pirated copies or unauthorized reprints. You can be confident that every purchase supports the original publishers and authors.

🔄 Returns & Support

If you receive a damaged or incorrect book, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery via WhatsApp with photos of the issue. We'll arrange a replacement or refund at no additional cost to you. We want every customer to be completely satisfied with their purchase.
